ABOUT

I'm a software engineer at Skyscanner, where I build the Android app that helps people find and book flights. I spent the last while deep in Kotlin, Jetpack, and the small details that make a mobile search experience feel instant — and I've recently started picking up iOS and Swift, moving toward working across both platforms.

I like the parts of mobile engineering that are easy to overlook: app startup time, state restoration, the moment a screen has to reflow because a network call came back slower than expected. Good travel software should feel calm even when the underlying systems are anything but.
